Academic Integrity

Our Commitment to Responsible Scholarship

Academic integrity is not a formality at Brain & Pen — it is the foundation of every service we provide. We are committed to supporting research that is honest, rigorous, and ethically sound.

Authorship & Attribution

We believe that authorship should reflect genuine intellectual contribution. Our role as a service provider is to support, structure, and refine — not to substitute for the author's own thinking and expertise. We encourage all clients to be transparent about professional support received, in accordance with their institution's and publisher's disclosure norms.

Citation & Source Integrity

Accurate and complete citation of sources is a non-negotiable standard in scholarly work. When we assist with literature reviews or manuscript preparation, we take care to ensure that sources are appropriately credited and that no material is presented as original when it draws on prior work.

Reproducible & Honest Analysis

Research support involving data analysis or statistical interpretation is carried out transparently. We do not fabricate, selectively omit, or misrepresent findings. Where methodology choices are made, we aim to explain and document them clearly.

Publication Ethics

We align our practices with widely recognised publication ethics standards, including those promoted by COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics). We do not support duplicate submissions, data manipulation, or any practice that would constitute research misconduct.

Institutional & Publisher Compliance

Different institutions and publishers maintain their own codes of conduct. It is the author's responsibility to ensure that the use of professional research support complies with these requirements. We are happy to discuss how our services fit within specific guidelines if asked.
