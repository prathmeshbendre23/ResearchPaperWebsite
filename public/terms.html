Terms of Engagement

Before working together, we believe it is important that clients understand what our services involve and what they do not. These terms reflect an honest description of how we work.

Nature of Services

Brain & Pen Research Services provides advisory and consultancy support for academic and professional research. Our services may include research structuring, literature support, writing guidance, statistical consultation, and manuscript preparation assistance, depending on the engagement.

Our role is that of a professional research support partner. The intellectual direction, subject matter expertise, and final responsibility for the work remain with the author.

No Publication Guarantees

We do not guarantee acceptance, publication, or specific editorial outcomes. Decisions regarding manuscript acceptance rest entirely with journals, conference committees, and publishers. Our support is aimed at improving the quality and presentation of your work, not at influencing editorial decisions.

Author Responsibility

The researcher or author engaging our services remains responsible for the accuracy of the research, the integrity of the data, compliance with their institution's guidelines, and all final submission decisions. We encourage all clients to disclose the use of professional editing or research support as required by their institution or target publication.

Scope & Agreements

The specific scope, deliverables, timeline, and any financial terms of an engagement are discussed and agreed upon directly with each client before work begins. There are no hidden obligations.

Amendments

These terms may be updated from time to time. The version on this page reflects our current practices.

Questions about scope or terms of a specific engagement? Write to us at brainandpen.research@gmail.com before proceeding.
