Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

Brain & Pen Research Services is committed to handling enquiry information with care and transparency. This page explains how information you share with us is used.

Information We Receive

When you reach out to us — through our contact form, email, or phone — you may share your name, contact details, and a brief description of your research needs. We use this information solely to understand your request and respond to it appropriately.

Purpose of Use

Any information you provide during the enquiry stage is used only to evaluate your research service request and to get back to you with relevant information. We do not use your contact details, including your phone number, for marketing, promotional messages, or any unsolicited communication.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your enquiry details remain within our team for the purpose of responding to you and, if you proceed, managing the service engagement.

Research Materials

Any research documents, drafts, or materials shared during consultation are handled with discretion and used only in the context of the service you have requested. Please also refer to our Confidentiality page for more detail.

Contact & Questions

If you have any questions about how your information is handled, please reach out to us directly at brainandpen.research@gmail.com. We are happy to clarify.
