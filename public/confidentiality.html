Confidentiality

Your Research Stays Yours

Researchers share sensitive materials with us as a matter of trust. We take that trust seriously. Everything shared during a consultation or engagement is handled with strict discretion.

What You Share With Us

During enquiry and service delivery, clients may share research drafts, datasets, analysis outputs, patent concepts, unpublished ideas, or other intellectual property. All such materials are received in strict confidence.

Enquiry-Stage Information

Information shared during the initial consultation — including your research topic, objectives, and any materials provided for scoping — is used solely to evaluate your request and determine how we can assist. It is not stored beyond the purpose of the engagement, nor shared externally.

During the Engagement

Materials and communications shared during an active engagement are used exclusively for delivering the agreed service. We do not repurpose, reference, publish, or share client work in any form without explicit written consent.

Intellectual Property

Ownership of all research materials, ideas, and outputs remains with the client. Engaging Brain & Pen for research support does not transfer any intellectual property rights. If there are specific IP considerations relevant to your engagement, we are happy to discuss them directly.

Our Team

Only the team members directly involved in your engagement will have access to your materials. We maintain internal standards to ensure that client information is not shared more broadly than necessary.

If you have specific confidentiality requirements or questions before sharing materials, please contact us at brainandpen.research@gmail.com.
