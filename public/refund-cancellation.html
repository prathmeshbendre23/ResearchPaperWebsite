Refund & Cancellation

Service Scope & Cancellation

Brain & Pen operates on the basis of direct, transparent conversations with each client. We believe the clearest way to address scope, payment, and cancellation is through honest discussion — not fine print.

How Engagements Are Structured

Before any work begins, we discuss the scope of the service, expected deliverables, timelines, and financial terms directly with you. Nothing is assumed or imposed. An engagement proceeds only when both parties are in agreement about what is being undertaken.

Cancellation Considerations

If you need to pause or cancel an engagement, we ask that you contact us as early as possible. Cancellation terms — including what has been completed, what is in progress, and any fair consideration for work already done — are worked out directly with each client based on the specifics of the situation.

We do not apply rigid, blanket cancellation or refund policies. Our approach is to have a straightforward conversation and reach a fair resolution.

Before You Engage

We strongly encourage prospective clients to clarify all expectations, including payment and cancellation terms, before confirming an engagement. If you have concerns or specific requirements in this area, please raise them upfront — we welcome the conversation.

To discuss scope, payment, or cancellation for an ongoing or prospective engagement, please reach out at brainandpen.research@gmail.com. We respond promptly.
